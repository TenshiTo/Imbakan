01 / THE MESSAGE

FOR RONNIE.

Some things deserve more than a quick “happy birthday” text.

Happy birthday, Ronnie. Another year older, another year of stories, mistakes, wins, random memories, late nights, stupid decisions, and moments that somehow became unforgettable.

I hope this next year gives you more than just another number added to your age. I hope it gives you experiences that actually mean something. I hope you get opportunities you never expected, meet people worth keeping around, and make memories you'll still be talking about years from now.

Life isn't always going to go exactly how you planned it. Sometimes everything works out. Sometimes everything goes completely off the rails. Either way, keep moving. Keep building yourself, keep learning, and keep becoming the kind of man you actually want to be.

I hope you win the battles nobody knows you're fighting. I hope the things stressing you out eventually become things you look back at and laugh about. I hope you have the courage to chase what you want, walk away from what isn't good for you, and appreciate what you already have.

Most importantly, don't forget to actually enjoy your life. Take the trip. Make the joke. Take the pictures. Stay out late once in a while. Tell people you appreciate them. Do things you'll remember. We don't get unlimited time, so make yours count.

Today is yours, man. Forget the stress for a bit, eat whatever the hell you want, enjoy the people around you, watch these embarrassing videos, and celebrate the fact that you've made it through another year.

Here's to more wins, more money, better days, good health, genuine people, ridiculous memories, and another year of becoming a better version of yourself.

HAPPY BIRTHDAY, RONNIE. 🤝
